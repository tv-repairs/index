Scope of Practice & Guidelines

Establishing and maintaining transparent ethical boundaries, radical client autonomy, and interdisciplinary coordination under MindBody guidelines.

1. Practice Philosophy & Target Population (The Continuum)

My name is Comrade Tom (he/they). I serve as an affirming, non-judgmental thinking partner for individuals navigating the tension between the life they have and the life they want.

The Wellness-Illness Paradigm

This practice operates explicitly on the bridge between clinical stability (asymptomatic) and holistic mental wellness. Services are designed for individuals who have achieved foundational psychological stability and possess the functional capacity and willingness to actively pursue personal growth, health optimization, and self-actualization.

Core Niche

Supporting individuals seeking to align their daily lives, careers, and actions with their internal wisdom. Specialized focus areas include:

  • Mind-Body Connection: Cultivating mind-body/somatic connection to inform energy-based and emotionally regulated decision-making.
  • Somatic Grounding: Establishing somatic grounding, meditation, and mindfulness practices rooted in felt safety.
  • Transitions & Health: Navigating career transitions, spiritual development, narrative well-being, and health management.
  • Equity & Action: Expanding intercultural capacity, dismantling dominant-identity blind spots, and operationalizing social justice values.

Identity-Affirming Focus

The primary population served is LGBTQ+ individuals, with a specific focus on those navigating the unique intersection of minority stress and chronic cultural or systemic invalidation.

Positionality & Cultural Humility

As a 39-year-old, cisgender, queer, white man, I actively recognize the unearned social capital and baseline trust my visible identities provide in professional spaces. I intentionally navigate inherent power dynamics by embracing cultural humility and centering the client as the primary authority of their own life.

3. Clear Boundaries of Practice (What Coaching Is and Is Not)

Clarifying the egalitarian boundaries between client-centered MindBody Coaching and clinical psychotherapy.

Coaching Scope (Within Practice)

The Non-Clinical Mandate: Coaching is an egalitarian partnership focused on exploring new ways of thinking, being, and experimenting with lifestyle changes. It is forward-facing and action-oriented.

  • Embodied & Relational Integration: Sourcing embodied, mindfulness-based, relational, and strengths-based approaches to support learning and development.
  • Personal Resource Access: Helping clients clarify goals, access personal resources, increase self-awareness, and develop actionable strategies.
  • Radical Autonomy & Trauma-Informed Care: Every tool, somatic practice, or mindfulness exercise is an offering, never a requirement. Clients retain complete authority over their bodies and participation (e.g., freedom to keep eyes open, choose alternate posture, or decline any exercise).
  • Pattern Inquiry & Modalities: Using the Enneagram, mind-body skills, and 12-step recovery principles to identify unconscious somatic reactions and default patterns.
  • Interdisciplinary Care Team: Centering yourself as the primary authority of your own life, while collaborating with your therapist (strictly with written consent) to align our habit-architecture with your clinical goals.

Clinical Scope (Outside Practice)

Strict Prohibitions: The coach does not diagnose mental health or medical conditions, prescribe treatment, or provide psychotherapy or other regulated clinical interventions.

  • No Clinical Diagnostics: Identifying, assessing, labeling, or diagnosing physical, mental, emotional, or psychiatric conditions.
  • No Clinical Psychotherapy: Treating clinical pathology, providing clinical counseling, or deeply processing acute clinical/developmental trauma wounds.
  • No Medical Prescriptions: Recommending, prescribing, adjusting, or advising on medications, medical treatments, or clinical interventions.
  • No Top-Down Directives: Giving clinical advice, imposing goals from a position of authority, or telling you what you "should" do.
  • Exceeding Competence: Proceeding with sessions when client needs fall outside my competence, without coordinating referrals.

4. Jurisdiction, Telepractice, & Privacy Protocols

  • 📍 Physical Location: Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA.
  • 🌐 Service Delivery: Primarily virtual (global telepractice), dependent on navigating cross-border or interstate regulations and time zones.
  • 🔒 Privacy & Data Security: Voluntarily aligning with and operating under HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) guidelines to ensure professional-grade management of Client Protected Health Information (PHI).
  • 🛡️ Secure Infrastructure: Video consultations are conducted via a secure, HIPAA-compliant Zoom license, and all client records and session notes are maintained inside a secure, HIPAA-compliant Google Workspace environment.

5. Risk Assessment, Red Flags, & Duty of Care

I maintain a strict threshold for client safety. A situation exceeds this coaching scope of practice if a client presents with:

  • Active mental health crises, acute psychiatric symptoms, or suicidal/homicidal ideation.
  • Inability to independently perform Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) due to mental, emotional, or physical distress.
  • An unmanaged medical or psychological condition where the client is not concurrently supported by a licensed healthcare professional.

Mandatory Duty of Care / Emergency Policy

While coaching lacks a uniform statutory mandate in all jurisdictions, this practice adopts a strict professional obligation regarding safety. If a client poses an imminent risk of serious harm to themselves or others, I will break confidentiality to execute emergency protocols, facilitate immediate crisis resources, or contact requisite emergency services.
